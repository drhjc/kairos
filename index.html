                      Use kairos on another device

                      Your plan doesn’t sync on its own. Here’s how to move it, for example from your laptop to your phone.

                      1. On the device with your plan: click Download backup file below.
                      2. Get the file to your other device. Save it to your USC OneDrive, email it to yourself, or AirDrop it.
                      3. On the other device: open kairos schedule bot, go to Settings, choose Choose backup file under “Restore from a backup,” and pick the file.

                      Changes you make on one device don’t show up on the other. Pick one device as your main one, or move your plan again after big changes. If downloading doesn’t work, use Copy backup code instead and paste it under “Or paste a backup code” on the other device.

                              Add your plan to your calendar

                              Downloads a calendar file you can import into Google, Apple, or Outlook calendar. Nothing is sent anywhere; the file is made on this device.

                              This is a snapshot of your plan right now. When your plan changes, export again. Tip: import into its own calendar, like “Study Plan,” so it’s easy to clear and replace.

                              How to import it
                              • Google Calendar: on a computer, open Settings, then Import & export, and choose kairos-calendar.ics. Google may use your own notification settings instead of the file’s reminders.
                              • Apple Calendar: on iPhone, open the file in the Files app and tap kairos-calendar.ics to add the events. On a Mac, double-click it.
                              • Outlook (your USC email): in Calendar, choose Add calendar, then Upload from file.
